# GKWorld360 — Hindi glossary

The ONE place for how names and technical terms are written in Hindi on GKWorld360, and where
each spelling comes from. Started 5 Nov 2026 after the owner asked "what is the authenticity of
these Hindi spellings?" — until then every Hindi word was Claude's own translation.

## Rules (owner, 5 Nov 2026)

1. **Source ladder** — names and technical terms come from, in this order: (1) NCERT Hindi
   textbooks, newest edition first; (2) CSTT, the government's Commission for Scientific and
   Technical Terminology (every subject, computers included); (3) the organisation's own Hindi
   website / PIB Hindi; (4) major Hindi newspapers, only as a last resort, flagged for the owner.
   Ordinary sentences need no source — only names and terms.
2. **The newest NCERT book wins, letter for letter** — including where it drops the nukta
   (मुगल, अंग्रेज, हजरत, एलिजाबेथ, जफ़र). Owner's choice, 5 Nov 2026.
3. **Official word + common word in brackets, at EVERY appearance** when the two differ a lot:
   भारत मंत्री (सेक्रेटरी ऑफ़ स्टेट), प्रचालन तंत्र (ऑपरेटिंग सिस्टम). Words that are already
   everyday Hindi stay as they are (कंप्यूटर, इंटरनेट).
4. A new article adds its terms here BEFORE its Hindi is written.

**Status key:** ✅ checked, our spelling matches the source · ✏️ checked, our spelling was
different — owner approved the list 5 Nov; corrected on dev 5 Nov, production after the owner checks dev · ⏳ not yet found in a source — still to check (next: NCERT
Class 7 *हमारे अतीत II* and Class 12 history in Hindi) · 🏛 from an official government source.

Page numbers are the printed page numbers in the book. NCERT PDFs: ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/<code>.pdf.
